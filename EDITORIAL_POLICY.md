# Editorial and AI Policy

## AI disclosure
Identity Field Notes is an AI-driven publication. Automation may collect, rank, cluster, summarize, tag and draft most site content. This is not hidden in bylines or buried in a terms page.

The operating slogan is: **I burn the tokens so you don't have to.**

## Guest Voices
Guest Voices is the human-authored lane of the publication. These pieces are written by named people and are labeled **HUMAN-WRITTEN** rather than AI summary.

Guest contributors may use AI as a writing or editing tool, but a real person must own the ideas, approve the final piece, and be willing to attach their name to it. Employment, vendor affiliation, consulting relationships, or other material commercial interests should be disclosed when relevant.

Guest Voices may include field experience, technical arguments, original research, architecture lessons, conference takeaways, interviews, and practitioner commentary. They should not be disguised vendor marketing or paid editorial placement.

Human-written pieces can appear in the same main feed and archive as automated Field Notes, but the authorship label must remain visually distinct.

## Source requirement
Every daily-news story should have a working source URL. Primary sources are preferred. Summaries are convenience layers, not evidence.

Guest opinion pieces do not need a source for every personal observation, but factual or technical claims should link to supporting material when practical.

## Corrections
Correct material errors visibly. Preserve discussion context when possible. Do not silently rewrite a material claim merely because the model made a mistake.

The same correction standard applies to human contributors.

## Sponsorship
Sponsored inventory is labeled. Sponsors receive placement, not favorable analysis or editorial control.

Payment cannot buy a Guest Voice designation, suppress criticism, or alter editorial ranking.

## Copyright
Summarize and link. Do not republish complete articles or substantial copyrighted passages.
