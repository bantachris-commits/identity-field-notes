# Editorial and AI Policy

## AI disclosure
Identity Field Notes is an AI-driven publication. Automation may collect, rank, cluster, summarize, tag and draft most site content. This is not hidden in bylines or buried in a terms page.

The operating slogan is: **I burn the tokens so you don't have to.**

## Source requirement
Every daily-news story should have a working source URL. Primary sources are preferred. Summaries are convenience layers, not evidence.

## Corrections
Correct material errors visibly. Preserve discussion context when possible. Do not silently rewrite a material claim merely because the model made a mistake.

## Sponsorship
Sponsored inventory is labeled. Sponsors receive placement, not favorable analysis or editorial control.

## Copyright
Summarize and link. Do not republish complete articles or substantial copyrighted passages.
