WHAT BELONGS HERE

Bring receipts.

Challenge an AI summary. Add a primary source. Explain how a control behaves in production. Share a vendor caveat. Correct a factual miss. A useful disagreement is more valuable than agreement without evidence.

GROUND RULES

Be useful.

Disagree freely. Add evidence. Disclose vendor affiliation when relevant. No sales ambushes, recruiting spam, doxxing, customer-confidential information or “DM me for pricing.”

LATER

Native community, if needed.

If GitHub login becomes a real barrier, the discussion UI can migrate to a native account system with email verification and moderation while leaving article URLs unchanged.
